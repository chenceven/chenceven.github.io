COUNTER-STRIKE 2

Five against five,
and nothing but decisions.

Counter-Strike 2 is a free-to-play tactical shooter on Steam, released in September 2023 on the Source 2 engine. Two teams of five take each other on round after round: plant the bomb or stop it, hold a site or take it back. Rounds are short, and one mistake is usually enough to lose one.

What keeps me coming back is not the shooting. It is that almost every round can be explained afterwards. Why did we lose that 3v2? Was the money spent badly two rounds earlier? Did we give away our position with a sound we did not even notice? The game always has an answer, and it is usually my fault.

At a glance

  • Format5v5, round based
  • PlatformSteam, free to play
  • EngineSource 2
  • RoundsMR12, first to 13

Where I play

  • Maps I likeInferno · Ancient

Three things I am learning

practice

01

Crosshair placement

Keeping the aim at head height before the fight starts. It looks like a small habit and it decides most duels.

02

Economy

Spending together, saving together. A half-buy that nobody agreed on is just a donation.

03

Utility

Smoke, flash, molotov — used to take space, not to look busy. One good flash beats five random ones.

“Aim is a skill. Not dying for free is a discipline.”
