Three decisions, in order

the brief
01

Structure first

Three pages that make sense on their own: the idea, the plan, and the people. Navigation on every page, so nobody has to guess where they are.

02

One stylesheet

All styling lives in styles.css. No inline styles, no second theme, no page that quietly breaks the rules the other two follow.

03

A story, not a template

A site should read like someone chose it. Dark, gold, slow — and honest about the parts that are still being worked on.
