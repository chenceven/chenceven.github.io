ABOUT

Still figuring it out.
Not standing still.

I am Chen Xiwen, a first-year student in Data Science and Big Data Technology. I did not choose this major because I had a plan for my life. I chose it because I like the feeling of a problem that looks impossible until the right method lands, and then the answer is just there.

Over the past year I started from zero with C, Python and the basics of calculus. The first program that actually ran, the first time a pile of numbers turned into a readable chart — that was more satisfying than any grade.

I am also clear about where I am weak: speaking in front of people. I get nervous and drop the point I wanted to make. This site is part of the practice — write it down, lay it out, put it out there, and hope someone else can follow it.
